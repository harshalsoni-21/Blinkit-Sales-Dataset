Data notes: Sourced from a single blinkit_customers extract. ~64% of rows were blank/incomplete from a bad merge and were excluded before analysis (2,500 of 6,852 rows retained). No product- or profit-level data exists in this source, so no margin figures are shown anywhere. Estimated Spend is a derived proxy (total_orders × avg_order_value), not actual transaction revenue — it's flagged wherever it appears. PII (name, email, phone, address) is excluded from every visual.
